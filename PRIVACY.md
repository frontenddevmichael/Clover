# Clover — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 16, 2026

## Overview

Clover is a study planning app for university students. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Data We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Full name
- Institution (university name)
- Department
- Academic level

### Academic Data
- Courses and course codes
- Class schedules and sessions
- Deadlines and assignments
- Semester dates

### Usage Data
- App interaction patterns (stored locally on your device)
- Offline queue status (synced when online)

## How We Use Your Data

- **Provide the service:** Your data is used to display your schedule, track deadlines, and power the AI study assistant.
- **Sync across devices:** When you're online, your data is synced to our secure backend (Convex) so it persists across sessions.
- **AI assistant:** If you choose to use the AI assistant, your schedule data is sent to Claude (Anthropic) to generate study plans. This data is not stored by Anthropic.

## Data Storage

- Your data is stored on Convex's secure cloud infrastructure.
- Session tokens are stored locally on your device using secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
- No data is sold to third parties.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties except:

- **Anthropic (Claude API):** Schedule data is sent temporarily for AI plan generation. Anthropic does not retain this data.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose data if required by law.

## Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Children's Privacy

Clover is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
